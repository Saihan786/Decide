# Decide

Decide is a web app for collaborative document review. A writer drafts a document and submits it to a group of reviewers, who each independently read through it, leave comments, and approve or disapprove with a reason. Once every reviewer has responded, the writer receives the consolidated outcome and all feedback.

No accounts or logins needed. The writer starts a session and shares a code with up to 10 reviewers, who join on their own devices and identify themselves by name — similar to how Kahoot works.

A session moves through three phases:

1. **Writing** — the writer composes the document at their own pace, across one or multiple sittings
2. **Review** — reviewers are notified the document is ready, and each submits their response independently
3. **Result** — the writer sees the overall outcome and all reviewer comments

The writer can optionally revise the document and send it out for another round of review.
