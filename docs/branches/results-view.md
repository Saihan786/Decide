# feature/results-view

## Goal
Show the writer the consolidated outcome once all reviewers have responded.

## Scope
- ☐ Writer sees overall approval/disapproval (e.g. 3 approved, 1 disapproved)
- ☐ Each reviewer's name, decision, reason, and comments are listed
- ☐ Session is marked complete

## Outcome
The writer has a clear, readable summary of all feedback and the overall outcome.
